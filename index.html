      How it works

      1. Watch. The pole runs an object detector on the existing CCTV feed and follows each vehicle and person from frame to frame.
      2. Score. Four signals come from those tracks: how much two boxes overlap, how much speed a vehicle lost in a fraction of a second, how far something turned, and whether a person is lying near a vehicle. They are weighted 0.4, 0.3, 0.2 and 0.1.
      3. Decide. A score of 0.85 or more dispatches on its own. Between 0.60 and 0.85 an operator decides. Each pole raises at most one alert every 30 seconds.
      4. Dispatch. The nearest ambulance and hospital are picked by straight-line distance, and the crew gets the pole's GPS pin.
      5. Help on site. Anyone at the pole can press First Aid for spoken, step-by-step instructions, and use the first aid box on the pole until the ambulance arrives.
      6. Restock. When the box is opened, the nearest hospital gets a request to refill it.

      On this page the camera, the detections and every ambulance and hospital are simulated. The scoring rules and the nearest-unit lookup are real code and run in your browser.
